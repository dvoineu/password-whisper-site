Support / Поддержка

For help with macOS, iOS, iCloud sync, browser extensions, or Pro purchases, email dvoineu@gmail.com.

По вопросам работы Password Whisper на Mac, iPhone, iPad, синхронизации iCloud и покупок пишите на dvoineu@gmail.com.

Enable Safari / System AutoFill

On macOS open System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions → Password AutoFill (on iOS: Settings → General → AutoFill & Passwords) and enable Password Whisper AutoFill.

Connect Chrome, Brave, Opera, or Firefox on Mac

Open Password Whisper → Settings → AutoFill & Browsers, click Connect next to your browser, and allow access to ~/Library/Application Support so Chrome or Firefox can talk to your sandboxed local Mac app.

Restore Purchases & Family Sharing

Your Password Whisper Pro subscription or Lifetime purchase works across Mac, iPhone, and iPad with the same Apple Account. Open Settings → Password Whisper Pro → Restore Purchases.
